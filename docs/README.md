# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, cross-functional project management lifecycle to turn ideas into delivered outcomes with clear ownership, measurable progress, and repeatable execution. The process starts with project initiation to validate demand, align stakeholders, and confirm whether work should move forward. Once approved, the team moves into planning to define scope, milestones, dependencies, and the backlog needed for delivery.

During execution, the team tracks progress through daily standups, sprint or milestone reviews, and structured project boards while maintaining quality through testing, reviews, and CI. Risk and communication are managed continuously so blockers, stakeholder updates, and dependencies are visible and resolved early. When a release is ready, OctoAcme follows a deployment checklist to validate readiness, reduce rollback risk, and communicate changes clearly. After each sprint or milestone, the team captures lessons learned through retrospectives and converts them into improvement actions. Finally, role clarity helps keep delivery aligned across developers, product managers, project managers, QA, and stakeholders.
