# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure products meet quality standards before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to optimize test coverage and testing efficiency.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy, test plans, and test cases aligned to acceptance criteria
- Execute manual testing and oversee automated test development
- Identify and triage defects, track quality metrics
- Validate release readiness through smoke tests and regression testing
- Collaborate with developers on test automation frameworks and CI/CD integration
- Participate in acceptance testing with product and stakeholders

### Goals
- Detect and prevent defects before production
- Maintain high test coverage and reduce rework cycles
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- Test plan reviews with developers and product teams
- Defect reports and quality metrics in weekly syncs
- Pre-release readiness checklist and sign-offs

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design solution and system architecture, define technical standards, and guide implementation to ensure scalability, performance, and maintainability. They work across teams to align technical decisions with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture and design for new features or systems
- Evaluate technical trade-offs and recommend approaches
- Establish and communicate coding standards and best practices
- Guide developers on design decisions and code quality
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Participate in design reviews and architecture decision records (ADRs)

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team velocity through clear technical guidance

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design reviews with development team
- Technical decision documentation (ADRs)
- Code review guidance and feedback
- Risk identification and escalation in planning and execution

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical delivery by analyzing requirements, defining scope, and ensuring solutions address customer and business objectives. They work with stakeholders, product managers, and developers to clarify requirements and manage change.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business requirements from stakeholders
- Define scope and document acceptance criteria
- Model workflows and identify process improvements
- Validate that solutions meet business needs
- Support change management and scope adjustments
- Facilitate communication between business and technical teams

### Goals
- Ensure clear understanding of requirements and expectations
- Reduce scope creep and rework through thorough analysis
- Deliver solutions that provide measurable business value

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements workshops
- Requirements documentation and scope specifications
- Weekly alignment with product managers and project leads
- Change request reviews and impact assessments

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate and execute product releases, ensuring deployment processes are smooth, well-documented, and minimize risk. They own release planning, communication, and coordination across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning and scheduling
- Prepare and maintain release notes and deployment documentation
- Coordinate with teams on readiness verification and smoke testing
- Manage deployment processes and rollback procedures
- Communicate release status and known issues to stakeholders and support teams
- Track deployment metrics and post-release verification

### Goals
- Execute reliable, predictable releases
- Minimize production incidents and rollbacks
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through transparent communication

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with product and engineering
- Pre-release checklists and readiness reviews
- Release notes and stakeholder communications
- Post-deployment verification and incident reports

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security and regulatory requirements, manage risk assessments, and guide secure development practices. They work with development and product teams to integrate security and compliance into all phases of delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Guide secure coding practices and architecture decisions
- Manage compliance audits and certifications
- Triage security incidents and coordinate response
- Provide security escalation paths and decision support

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Enable secure, compliant product delivery
- Maintain organizational security posture and customer trust

### Typical Communication
- Security architecture reviews and threat analysis sessions
- Compliance requirement documentation and audit reports
- Security incident escalation and response coordination
- Risk registers and security decision logs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When forming project teams, refer to these personas to ensure critical functions are assigned and communication patterns are established.
